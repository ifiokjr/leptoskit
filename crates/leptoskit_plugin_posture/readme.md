# leptoskit_plugin_posture

How a foldable phone is folded right now, for native and browser apps: closed on its cover screen, half open like a laptop or a book, or open flat, with every hinge that crosses the app's window. Select `posture` in `native.plugins` to register the Swift, Kotlin, and ArkTS handlers. Android adds `androidx.window:window` to the generated app; no permission is needed.

A `Posture` has an overall `fold`, whether the device is `foldable`, and up to four `Hinge`s. Each hinge reports its own fold, its axis, the region where it crosses the window as fractions of the window, its angle in whole degrees, and whether it hides content beneath it. `Posture::panes` splits the window around the hinges, so a layout can put content on either side of a fold with percentage sizes. `is_tabletop` and `is_book` name the two half-open postures. `leptoskit_use::use_posture` turns a posture signal into memoized queries.

`guest::DevicePosture::watch(on_change)` and `web::watch(on_change)` report the current posture and every change after it until dropped. `wait_for_change(previous)` returns the first posture that differs; intermediate states can coalesce, so this is the latest state, not a transition log. One wait can be outstanding per plugin; a second receives `Busy`.

Trifolds report one hinge per fold that crosses the window. A trifold showing two of its three panels is open, with its third hinge closed and left out. Android reports the hinges WindowManager reports; whether a particular trifold reports one folding feature per hinge depends on its vendor.

## Wire contract

All words are little-endian u32. A posture is a foldable flag, the fold, and the hinge count (at most 4), then nine words per hinge: fold, axis, occluding flag, region flag, the region's x, y, width, and height as `f32` bits, and the angle in degrees. Folds are unknown/closed/half open/open = 0/1/2/3; axes are unknown/vertical/horizontal = 0/1/2; flags are unknown/false/true = 0/1/2; an unknown angle is `u32::MAX`; an absent region is zeros. Regions must lie inside the unit square and angles must not exceed 360. Truncated, trailing, and unknown values are rejected.
