# Wall-clock times, durations, and sequences

Columns can now hold a `chrono::NaiveDateTime` or `chrono::NaiveTime` — a calendar's date and time or a time of day in no time zone, `timestamp` and `time` on Postgres and ISO 8601 text on SQLite — and a `chrono::Duration`, stored as ISO 8601 duration text (`PT90M`, `-PT36H`) on both databases, so a grace period or a lead time round-trips exactly, sign and fraction included. `#[db(sequence)]` on an `i64` makes the database number the rows in order (`GENERATED BY DEFAULT AS IDENTITY` on Postgres); inserts don't name the column, and SQLite projects are told to key by an `Id` or keep a counter of their own, with a way out in the message.
