# Access policies on tables

`#[db::policy(select, |article, cx| …)]` below `#[db::table]` declares who may read, insert, update, or delete rows, as a condition that can read the request's globals: any value in a `Context`, found by type, such as `cx.global(|viewer: &Viewer| article.author_id.eq(viewer.0))`. Once a table has a policy, only what a rule allows is allowed, and a `deny` wins. Reads, updates, and deletes skip rows the request may not touch. A refused insert, or an update whose result isn't allowed, fails with `DbError::Forbidden` and the rule's message, and nothing is written. `db.with_context(cx)` scopes a handle to a request. A plain `Db` acts for an empty context, and `db.system("reason")` bypasses policies explicitly.
